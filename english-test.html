Reading comprehension - A mobile 2D project

Lighthouse Runner

Three learners had five days to create a small 2D mobile game. The player controls a courier who must collect signal batteries and reach a lighthouse before nightfall. The team first made one scene with a static camera to test movement and collisions. They then added a dynamic camera and touch controls.

Nora imported the character sprites, backgrounds and sound effects. Sam programmed movement, battery collection and the life counter. Ilyes connected the levels and made the score and remaining lives persist when the player changed scenes.

The game worked well on a recent phone but was slow on an older device because too many particle effects appeared at the same time. The team reduced the particles and compressed several textures. They also added larger text, stronger contrast and movable touch buttons.

Before the presentation, they documented the controls, main scripts, known limitations and possible future improvements.
